# CONTEXT.md

This file is the **domain glossary** of the ISO/TC 154 website project —
the vocabulary that names the seams. Every architecture review starts
here; every PR description that names a concept should be derivable
from a term defined in this file.

## The committee

- **ISO/TC 154** — "Processes, data elements and documents in commerce,
  industry and administration". The committee this website serves.
  Secretariat currently SAC (China); chair Ms. Pan (Megan) Wei
  (CalConnect).
- **Working group (WG)** — a long-lived body that develops a family of
  standards (e.g. WG 5 owns the ISO 8601 family). Currently active:
  WG 2 (VTL), WG 5 (date/time), WG 6 (trusted communications), WG 7
  (digital business), JWG 1 (UNECE / UN/EDIFACT), JWG 9 (ISO 7372 /
  TDED / UNTDED).
- **Joint working group (JWG)** — co-sponsored with another committee;
  the same shape as a WG.
- **Study group (SG)** — a short-lived body exploring a new topic
  before a formal project exists (e.g. SG 1 on "Standardization
  content and document", P-2025-01).
- **Ad-hoc group (AHG)** — an even shorter body for a specific
  incubation (e.g. the "incubator proposal" group of P-2026-09).
- **Convenor** — the chair of a WG/JWG/SG; their term is renewed by
  plenary resolutions (e.g. P-2026-11 extends three terms).
- **Secretariat** — the body that runs the committee (currently SAC);
  receives and circulates documents.
- **Chair's Advisory Group (CAG)** — internal advisory body.

## The decision model

- **Decision** — a formal outcome of a meeting: a resolution, order,
  ruling, determination, recommendation, statement, finding, opinion, or
  other. The registry carries the first; the Edoxen schema allows the
  others.
- **Resolution** — the decision kind every TC 154 plenary/ballot
  outcome uses (`kind: resolution`). The registry's TYPE facet
  therefore only renders `resolution`.
- **Numbering** — plenary resolutions carry a `P-YYYY-NN` identifier
  (e.g. P-2026-13); ballot (CIB) resolutions carry a `V-YYYY-NN`
  identifier (e.g. V-2026-06).
- **Appreciation as decision** — expressions of thanks/memorials are
  recorded as resolutions (`P-2026-12`–`P-2026-15`), not as prose.
  Their action verb follows the house convention `thanks` (plenary-44
  precedent); `appreciates` was used by some older files and
  normalized to `thanks` in the review-10 verb-taxonomy fix.
- **Action verb house set** — the verbs in legitimate registry use,
  pinned in `src/lib/registry.pages.spec.ts`. Twelve render in the
  facet top-12; the rest are established but under the cutoff.

## Decision registry and URNs

- **Decision registry** — the `/decisions/` site, rendered by
  `@edoxen/browser` from decisions-data (see Integration below).
- **Decision URN, registry namespace** —
  `urn:iso:tc154:resolution:{source}:{id}` (e.g.
  `urn:iso:tc154:resolution:plenary-45:P-2026-13`). New data uses
  this colon form.
- **Decision URN, events-edoxen namespace** —
  `urn:iso-tc154:meeting:plenary-{n}` (dash form, for *meetings*).
  Carried in `_data/events-edoxen/*.yaml`; rendered by the legacy
  redirect path. Do **not** confuse with the decision URN form.
- **Legacy redirect** — `/resolutions/plenary/plenary-NN/P-YYYY-NN/`
  redirects to `/decisions/urn:iso:tc154:resolution:plenary-NN:P-YYYY-NN/`,
  generated by `scripts/build-legacy-redirects.mjs` from the submodule
  YAML; 654 entries as of the 45th plenary.

## The member model

- **Member** — a named expert on the committee's member card.
- **P-member** — a participating national body, votes.
- **O-member** — an observing national body, no vote.
- **Affiliation** — a free-text organization name (`DIN, Germany`,
  `OAGi`, …).
- **Seat** — which organization a member **holds a seat for** through a
  liaison role (e.g. `Seat: ISO`, `Seat: UNECE`); carried on role
  records, not on the member top level. Five members currently carry
  seats; rendered as a chip per role row on the member detail page
  since the review-11 seat-rendering fix.
- **Liaison organization** — the body the liaison representative comes
  from (ISO, UNECE, WCO, …); represented in the site data through
  `src/data/liaisons/` and the `Liaison` typed-seam entry.
- **Liaison representative** — the individual member holding the seat;
  recorded as a role record with `seat: <org>`.
- **Convenor role** — a special role id with its own role-record shape.
- **Deceased member** — `active: false` + `deceased: true` + every role
  closed `to:` the date of passing (Morton/Borges convention).
  `active: false` is **authoritative** in the pipeline: an open role
  cannot resurrect a closed membership.
- **In Memoriam** — the section on the member page that renders the
  `epitaph` field (added by review 10; previously data-only, invisible).
- **Epitaph** — the In Memoriam body text. Optional; rendered only when
  the member is deceased.

## Standardization stages (ISO/IEC Directives Part 1)

`PWI` (Preliminary Work Item) → `NP` (New Proposal) → `CD`
(Committee Draft) → `DIS` (Draft International Standard) → `FDIS`
(Final Draft International Standard) → `IS` (International Standard).
Amendments: `ISO 8601-1:2019/Amd 1:2022` etc.
Fast-track: the `F.2` clause path used by P-2026-02.

## Document classes

- **N-document** — an ISO/TC 154 numbered document (e.g. N 1727
  carries the 45th plenary resolutions); archived under
  `reference-docs/` in the submodule.
- **Strategic Business Plan (SBP)** — a special long-lived document
  with editions (2019 N 1106, 2020 N 226, 2001 public review);
  rendered under `/business-plan/` with per-edition pages.
- **Reference-docs** — the original PDFs/DOCs the YAML was transcribed
  from. **Never edit or delete** even after transcription.
- **Legacy preservation** — `legacy/{plenary,ballots,7372ma}/`
  mirrors pre-migration originals; **never edit or delete**.
- **TDED** — Trade Data Elements Directory (ISO 7372); jointly maintained
  with UNECE. Resolutions live in the `7372ma/` tree (currently only
  the 2002 founding file) and the plenary's JWG 9 sections
  (P-2026-06, -07, -08).

## Submodule and data pipeline

- **resolutions-data** — git submodule at `_data/resolutions`,
  https://github.com/iso-tc154/resolutions-data. Edits flow through
  `branch → bundle exec edoxen normalize --inplace → edoxen validate →
  PR → rebase-merge → parent repin`. The parent rebuilds
  `legacy-redirects.json` automatically.
- **Meetings duality** — `data/meetings.yml` is the canonical
  xlsx-derived list (all plenaries); `_data/events/plenary-meeting-NN.yml`
  adds rich per-meeting content (host, venue, agenda, deadlines).
  `_data/events-edoxen/` is the generated edoxen event view, never
  hand-edited.
- **Edoxen model 1.0** — the YAML schema for decisions (per-field
  `LocalizedString[]` shape); schema lives at edoxen-model, validated
  by the `edoxen` Ruby gem.

## Astro integration

- **@edoxen/browser** — the decision-registry renderer; lives entirely
  inside `/decisions/`. Hosted by `src/integrations/edoxen-host.ts`
  which disables the package's own route injection and re-injects only
  the two decisions routes (`/decisions`, `/decisions/[urn]`).
- **Theme bridge** — the inline script the host integration injects
  that keeps the two storage keys (`theme` site / `edoxen-theme`
  browser) and the two DOM toggles (`<html class="dark">` vs
  `<html data-theme="dark">`) in sync. Without it, the two halves
  remember different themes.
- **decisions-data submodule** — see above.
- **@edoxen/browser check** — the CI gate that runs after
  `prepare-edoxen-data` + `generate-edoxen-events` (the latter two
  populate gitignored staging directories).
