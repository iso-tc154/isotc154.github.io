Tomorrow, Monday 31 August 2026, ISO/TC 154 convenes its 45th plenary week
in Berlin, Germany, hosted by DIN at Am DIN-Platz, Burggrafenstraße 6.
The week runs through Friday 4 September 2026.

== A full week of work

The updated meetings schedule (N 1709) brings all working groups together
around the plenary:

* **31 Aug** — Plenary opening session (9:00–10:30), followed by JWG 1,
  WG 2 and SG 1 in the afternoon
* **1 Sep** — JWG 9 (UNTDED work) and WG 6 (trusted communications)
* **2 Sep** — WG 7 day session (digital business), plus the social
  programme in the evening
* **3 Sep** — TC-level projects meeting (including the path forward for
  ISO/PWI 26087 after the first NP ballot) and WG 5 (date and time)
* **4 Sep** — (J)WG reports to the plenary in the morning, closing
  session from 14:30

The plenary itself is hybrid — delegates can join via Zoom (meeting ID
850 446 6562).

== The agenda

The updated draft agenda (N 1711) discusses items 1–11 on 31 August —
from the management report with a full round of ballot results through
to a new item 11: a presentation of the research project
"https://www.iso.org[Rising Standards Powers in Global Technical
Standard-Setting]" by Professor Sarah Eaton and Mr Nicolas Huppenbauer.

Items 12–23 follow on 4 September: working group reports, liaison
reports, strategic review of the Business Plan, and the approval of
resolutions.

== Social programme

On Wednesday evening (2 September), the committee is invited to an
exclusive evening tour of Zoo Berlin — just across the street from the
DIN building. Meet at 18:30 at DIN; the tour starts at 19:00.
Afterwards, everyone interested can continue to the nearby Biergarten
"Café am Neuen See".

Registration for the social event is via the
https://sd.iso.org/meetings/182492[ISO meeting platform].
