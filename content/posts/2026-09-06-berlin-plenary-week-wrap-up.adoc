The 45th plenary week of ISO/TC 154 has come to a close — five productive
days at DIN in Berlin, from Monday 31 August to Friday 4 September 2026,
with working group meetings throughout the week and the plenary session
bracketing it.

== Danke schön, DIN

Our deepest thanks go to our host, **DIN**, and the entire DIN delegation,
for organizing and running the week so effectively and efficiently — from
the meeting rooms at Am DIN-Platz to the seamless hybrid setup that kept
our remote colleagues with us throughout. Our particular appreciation to
Mr. Gregor Roschkowski and Ms. Kristina Schönebeck for preparing and
supporting the meetings, as the plenary recorded in its
link:/decisions/urn:iso:tc154:resolution:plenary-45:P-2026-15/[appreciation
resolution].

Thank you as well to our convenors, whose preparation made the week's
discussions so productive, and to all the participants — those who
travelled to Berlin and those who joined online, in some cases during
unearthly hours. Your engagement is what makes the committee's work.

== A week of results

The plenary adopted fifteen resolutions, now recorded in the
link:/decisions/[decision registry]: from the fast-track of the VTL
specification into DIS ballot, nine-month extensions for the ISO 8601-1
and ISO 8601-2 revisions and ISO 20197-2, the establishment of the
"incubator proposal" ad-hoc group, liaison representatives to
ISO/IEC JTC 5, and the committee's appreciations — including a
link:/members/rudolf-borges/[memorial to Dr. Rudolf Borges], our
long-time WG 5 expert who passed away earlier this year.

The full briefing for the week remains on the
link:/meetings/45/[45th plenary meeting page].

== Pandas! A Wednesday evening at Zoo Berlin

The social programme took us across the street on Wednesday evening for
an exclusive tour of **Zoo Berlin** — and what a treat it was. The stars
of the evening were, of course, the **pandas**, enjoying their evening
bamboo while we crowded the viewing window. Beyond the pandas, the tour
wandered past numerous other animals of one of the world's most
species-rich zoos — a genuinely memorable Berlin evening for everyone
who came along. Our thanks to DIN for arranging it.

== See you online

With the resolutions adopted and the working groups' roadmaps set for
the months ahead, we now return to our (virtual) desks. We look forward
to seeing everyone again in the online meetings soon — may they be as
productive as this week in Berlin.

Danke, Berlin — until next time.
