[NOTE]
====
This is the earliest published Business Plan record in this archive. The
2001 Business Plan — document N 367 — was circulated for public review by
the then chair, Mr François Vuilleumier, on 2 August 2001, with comments
due to Mr Jean Kubler by 2001-10-19. The original plan files were hosted
on the UN/UNECE FTP service, which no longer exists; the announcement
below is preserved verbatim. The plan text itself is not in this archive.
====
